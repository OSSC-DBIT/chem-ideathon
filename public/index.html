How it works

  1. Pick an eventEach student can take part in one event, so choose well.

  3. Pay by UPIScan the QR, pay the exact amount, then enter the transaction ID and upload the screenshot.
  4. Get confirmedThe organisers verify the payment. Track it any time with your registration ID. Winners get prize money, and everyone gets a certificate.
