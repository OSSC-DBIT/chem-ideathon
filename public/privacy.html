Privacy policy

Ideathon '26 registration site. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

This site is run for the Department of Chemistry, Don Bosco Institute of Technology (DBIT), Bengaluru, to take registrations for Ideathon '26. It is built and maintained by the OpenSource Students Club (OSSC), DBIT. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • For every team member: full name, roll number, branch and section. A phone number is optional for members.
  • For the team leader: phone number and email address as well.
  • Payment proof: the UPI transaction ID and the payment screenshot you upload.
  • Student list: the college gives the organisers a list of 1st year students (roll number, name, branch, section, and where available phone and email). It is used to fill in your name from your roll number and to check that a registration really comes from you.
  • Email verification: the team leader may be sent a one time code by email. The code is not kept after it is used or expires.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly, in memory, to limit abuse of the form. It is not stored with your registration.

Why we collect it

  • To register your team for an event and prepare participant lists and attendance sheets.
  • To verify your payment against the bank statement of the collecting account.
  • To contact the team leader about the registration or the event.

We do not use your details for advertising, and we do not sell or share them with anyone outside the college. Emails (verification codes and updates about your registration) are delivered through an email provider, which handles the leader's email address and the message in order to deliver it. Uploaded files are checked by a virus scanner that runs on the same college server.

Who can see it

  • The event organisers in the Department of Chemistry, through a password protected admin panel. Each organiser has their own account.
  • The OSSC members who maintain this site and its server.

Admin actions such as approvals, rejections and data exports are recorded in an activity log.

Where it is stored and for how long

All data is stored on a server operated by OSSC inside the college, and is encrypted on disk: the database and every payment screenshot. Registration data and payment screenshots are kept until the event is over and its accounts are settled, and are then deleted.

Teammates' details

The team leader enters details for the whole team. If you are a leader, get your teammates' permission before you submit their names and roll numbers.

Cookies and tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising and no third party trackers. Your browser's local storage is used to keep an unfinished form and the registration IDs created on your device, so that you do not lose them on refresh. That data stays in your browser. A login cookie is set only for organisers who sign in to the admin panel.

Your choices

You can ask the organisers to correct or delete your registration at any time. Contact the event coordinator, Prof Chinmayi G, Department of Chemistry, DBIT, or reach OSSC through webuildnow.in.
